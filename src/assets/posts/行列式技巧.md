$$
D=\begin{vmatrix}
-a & b & c & d \\
b & a & -d & c \\
c & d & a & -b \\
d & -c & b & a
\end{vmatrix}
$$
$D^{2}=D\times D^{t}=(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2})^{4}$
